Why these matter
Protein
Builds and repairs muscle, keeps you fuller for longer, and helps protect muscle mass — especially important in a calorie deficit or as you get older.
Carbohydrates
Your body's main energy source, fuelling workouts, daily activity and brain function. Wholegrain, fruit and veg sources also bring fibre and vitamins.
Fat
Supports hormone production and helps absorb vitamins A, D, E and K. It's essential for long-term health and shouldn't be cut too low.
